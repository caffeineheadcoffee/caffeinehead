IT Health Check

We conduct a comprehensive assessment of an organization's IT infrastructure and systems to verify their effective and secure operation. Our evaluation encompasses network security, data protection protocols, system performance, software and hardware vulnerabilities, and overall IT governance. Through the identification of potential weaknesses and areas for improvement, we assist organizations in fortifying their IT security, improving system performance, and implementing best practices to establish a dependable and robust IT environment.

VAPT Service

We aid organizations in recognizing and addressing security vulnerabilities within their Information Systems Assets. Our approach involves a methodical vulnerability assessment aimed at revealing weaknesses in networks, servers, applications, and crucial components. Following the assessment, we perform manual penetration testing, mimicking real-world attacks to exploit vulnerabilities and assess existing security measures. Through Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT), we support organizations in strengthening their security stance, safeguarding sensitive data, and proactively managing cyber threats.

Managed Security Service

We offer ongoing oversight, administration, and swift response to security incidents for organizations. Functioning as a Managed Security Service Provider (MSSP), we play a crucial role in overseeing and securing various facets of an organization's digital infrastructure, including networks, systems, applications, and data. Utilizing advanced security tools and technologies, we actively identify and thwart potential threats in real-time, such as intrusion attempts, malware, and unauthorized access. Additionally, we contribute to the analysis of security logs, generate reports, and assist in incident response and recovery efforts. Through our provision of managed security services, our goal is to fortify the organization's security stance and provide reassurance by effectively addressing security challenges.

SIEM Solution

We present a comprehensive platform that empowers organizations to collect, correlate, and analyze security events and data from diverse sources. Our Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) solution gather log data from networks, systems, applications, and security devices, facilitating centralized visibility and monitoring. Through the application of advanced analytics and machine learning algorithms, we pinpoint patterns, detect anomalies, and generate alerts for potential security incidents. Our SIEM solution ensures real-time threat detection, incident response, and forensic investigation capabilities, aiding organizations in proactively identifying and addressing security risks. With a centralized and holistic view of security events, we contribute to improved situational awareness, faster incident response, and an overall enhanced security posture.

SWIFT CSP Assessment

We deliver comprehensive services in SWIFT CSP (Customer Security Programme), focusing on evaluating and improving the security and compliance of financial institutions utilizing the SWIFT network for financial transactions. Our assessment involves a thorough examination of the organization's existing security controls, policies, and procedures to ensure alignment with the SWIFT CSP framework. We perform vulnerability assessments, penetration testing, and security architecture reviews to pinpoint potential weaknesses and gaps in security. Additionally, we provide recommendations and guidance to fortify security measures, implement necessary controls, and enhance the overall cybersecurity posture. Essentially, our goal is to assist financial institutions in meeting the rigorous security requirements outlined by SWIFT, thereby safeguarding their financial transactions and mitigating potential threats and unauthorized access.

Digital Forensic Service

We specialize in the methodical investigation, analysis, and preservation of electronic evidence following a data breach. Our services cover the scrutiny of digital devices, including computers, smartphones, storage media, and networks. We undertake activities such as data recovery, authentication, and analysis to gather information usable in legal proceedings. This involves tasks such as data recovery, data analysis, password cracking, metadata examination, and timeline reconstruction. Leveraging specialized tools and techniques, we identify, preserve, and interpret digital evidence, ensuring the integrity and admissibility of the data in the investigative process.
